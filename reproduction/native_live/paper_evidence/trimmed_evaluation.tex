\paragraph*{Complete request economics}
Degree prefix at budget 500 uses 421,246 requests across 96 cells,
saving 9.3\% against exact right-first (83 wins, 13 losses) and 8.9\% against the retrospective minimum
of five exact controls. Paid cut at budget 125 saves 6.0\% against that
diagnostic. The added dynamic-degree and negative-first controls use
155,445 and 156,496 pooled oracle requests versus right-first's 154,789;
negative-first also buys 160,000 helper scores. Full rows of the
earlier prefix/cut and no-audit matrices are retained in the artifact.

\paragraph*{Validation and coverage}
The primary matrix contains 1,376 executions: seven no-audit controls
per target and six audited policies at each of six seed/budget settings.
All run single-threaded on Windows 11 with DuckDB 1.5.6, NumPy 2.5,
and Python 3.13. Every returned bag passes independent SQL checks;
448 affine identities and 384 paired request-set inclusions are verified.
Eight method records have count-coverage failures and seven freeze after
inconsistency; none is removed, and no returned bag exceeds loss 0.1.
On the separate 12-query natural roster, 50 new audit seeds per query
at each of budgets 125/500 give 1,200 simultaneous-coverage events,
with no observed failure. Events share fixed targets and can share draws;
they are descriptive calibration checks, counted once per query/seed/budget.







\paragraph*{Budgeting, feedback, and audit cost}
At budget 500, the global helper certificate saves 2.36\% over its
matched per-key control (80 wins, 16 ties). Cut feedback at 125 saves
4.95\% over the same-start frozen residual. Helper-guided direct
ordering uses more requests than metadata ordering in 191/192 cells.
The no-audit degree control uses 145,645 requests; auditing at 500
reduces its matched pooled cost by 3.59\% (72 wins, 24 losses).
Raising the paid-cut budget from 125 to 500 instead increases requests.

At helper/oracle token-price ratio 0.1, budget-125 helper direct costs
8.0\% more than exact scan; paid cut saves 1.3\%. Median local algorithm
times are 248\,ms for degree direct at 500 and 200\,ms for cut at 125,
using one warmup and three repeats. SQL output and full-reference
verification are timed separately.

\paragraph*{Value of exact allocation}
DP reduces greedy's complete requests by $0.29\%$ (greedy versus DP
W/T/L $9/18/21$). On the earlier 48 native cells the means are 8,066 and
8,065 requests. Recorded median whole-policy CPU is 7.40\,s for greedy
versus 11.85\,s for DP; the median paired reduction is $38.84\%$.
Greedy is the practical policy, and DP provides the fixed-cache optimum
and an allocation-quality reference.

Against the original 65-position degree-prefix grid, all-mask feedback
pays 208,178 versus 212,508 requests at audit 125 and 197,563 versus
204,959 at audit 500: savings $2.04\%$/$3.61\%$, with W/T/L $36/4/8$
and $42/2/4$. A 48-cell budget-500 diagnostic evaluates every integer
degree-prefix cardinality with the same audits, ranking, batches, and
pruning. Dense prefix pays 204,534 requests, $0.21\%$ below the grid;
all-mask still saves $3.41\%$ (W/T/L $37/7/4$, worst regression 248
requests). Greedy count saves $3.13\%$ against dense prefix. This
diagnostic retains one shared uncovered audit and its conflict freezes,
whose final support is undefined.

Initial all-mask discard cardinality weakly dominates the matched prefix
in every cell, as the snapshot theorem requires. Complete adaptive cost
can reverse that ordering: without auditing, all-mask costs $4.1\%$ more
than degree prefix (74,616 versus 71,670 requests; W/T/L $5/2/9$).
Savings over exact right-first reach $39.3\%$/$32.9\%$ on the two
highest-fan-out targets, but the fan-out gradient disappears against
degree prefix, attributing that pattern to ordering rather than allocation.
The artifact reports each of the seven exact controls separately on constructed and native targets.

\paragraph*{When certification constrains execution}
The initial median certified discard is 9.2\% of unknowns. Three of
48 cells have zero support and hold the mask for two to four rounds;
new facts then relax the bound. At termination every remaining unknown
can be certified discarded. Maximum loss is 6.5\% with budget 500 and
9.2\% without auditing.
On \texttt{athlete}--\texttt{sports\_team}, a low-fan-out side is
72/72 positive: count allocation protects 31 of those identities while
discarding near-empty keys (0.71\% positive), instead of degree prefix's
138 initial incident discards. On
\texttt{company}--\texttt{educational\_institution}, an uncovered region
causes a freeze; all-mask's seed-specific savings are
9.4\%/7.2\%/$-5.4\%$, retained as off-coverage outcomes.





All 112 all-mask executions independently materialize and validate the returned SQL bag. Their maximum observed loss is 8.02\%, with 1 observed uncovered record and 0 conflict freezes. The mechanism records contain 3 observed uncovered records, including repeated use of the same initial audit across controls. Every returned bag in these follow-ups meets the 10\% loss allowance. Coverage is specified per audit source, with a 0.95 per-query target. Loss is measured against the pinned model tape. At budgets 125/500, median all-mask local algorithm time is 2.661/1.560\,s, excluding inference.



The original enumerating builder scales to $N=131{,}072$, $K=8/128$
and uniform/Zipf(1.5) sizes (artifact planner-scale records).
Empty query caches retain equal-state reuse within the query. At the
largest $N$, median times are 0.535/9.59\,s for $K=8$ and
0.0097/6.28\,s for $K=128$; traced allocations peak at 21.43\,MiB.



A controlled grid crosses $N=128/512/2048$, $K=2/8/32$, both size laws,
and nominal interval widths $0/.1/.3/.6/1$, with the midpoint fixed at
half the block and integer endpoints rounded outward. All 90
configurations and two key permutations are retained. Exact joint
allocation permits more discards in 45 configurations, by up to
3.125\% of $N$, and never fewer (artifact certificate-grid records).
At full uncertainty, both certify zero discards. Allocation quality
remains separate: the shared greedy heap discards fewer under joint
profiles in four of the 180 permutation runs.
