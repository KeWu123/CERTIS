# ICDE 2027 contribution-strengthening study

This is an exploratory extension after all earlier outcomes were inspected. It is not preregistered or held-out confirmation. Earlier inputs and results remain unchanged.

1. On the same 16 eligible constructed targets, seeds 101/202/303 and audit budget 500 per side, compare fixed-grid, adaptive-grid, fixed-continuous and adaptive-continuous per-side certificates. Adaptive policies maximize additional discards subject to containing every previously discarded identity. The continuous policy searches all integer discard-count pairs exactly, with no split grid and exact rational 90% product recall. All arms use the same original SRS audit, stable degree execution, batches of 128, positive rescue, partner elimination and complete SQL materialization. Existing joint-greedy and joint-DP records supply the separately identified historical comparators. No adverse cell is removed.

2. Natural contrast tasks use the original 4,998-comment GoEmotions subreddit/thread relationships and six semantically chosen predicate pairs: positive/negative, approval/disapproval, joy/sadness, optimism/fear, love/disgust, gratitude/disappointment. These pairs are chosen before the new executions, without testing outcomes. Keep both key types, seeds 101/202/303 and budgets 125/500. Measure the practical joint-greedy policy, adaptive continuous per-side policy and batched exact left/right execution. Exact controls perform the same possible-partner elimination, rechecking after each batch; they receive no free labels. Their batched execution can acquire labels a sequential control would skip, which is recorded as a scheduling cost. Retain earlier sequential exact controls as a separate reference.

3. Record oracle acquisition rounds explicitly and count audit/execution union, input/output tokens, planning and audit CPU/wall time, SQL output materialization and excluded reference-check time separately. Cost boundaries use the actual recorded batches, not a sum of historic API latencies. A local live-model benchmark, if available, is an explicitly separate hardware/model scenario: its timings and generated labels cannot be attributed to the paper's Llama-3.3-70B tape. If the exact oracle service is unavailable, report that missing measurement and the required latency/price thresholds, without inventing a 70B end-to-end speedup.

4. Verify the closed-form per-side bound and continuous count optimizer against exhaustive Boolean enumeration on small domains, including zero-answer and forced-discard cases. Independently recheck final supports, paid identities, retained completion and literal DuckDB bags. Freeze source/input hashes before each production stage; any source change requires a new stage identifier. Preserve pilot and failed records.

5. Rewrite the paper around the certificate and practical greedy allocation; use DP as the fixed-cache optimal reference. Update all abstract/introduction/result numbers to the stronger comparator, including if the earlier 15.10% advantage disappears. Keep the approved equal-size Figures 3/4 and the double-column table design, adapting their data if needed. Do not change formal theorem statements unless a real correctness issue is found.
