# Third-party data

`b102ced758428a47191a2a83f1890e44bb715e41`:

https://github.com/semviews/lotus-semviews/tree/b102ced758428a47191a2a83f1890e44bb715e41

Its original Apache-2.0 license is retained at
`reproduction/runtime/candidate_systems/external/semviews/LICENSE`.
The original `docs/DATA.md` and download manifest document the datasets,
recorded oracle/helper interfaces, upstream licenses, URLs, and checksums.
DBpedia inputs are identified as CC0-1.0 and GoEmotions inputs as Apache-2.0
in that manifest. These third-party attributions and licenses are retained.

The restored discussion relationship metadata uses the original GoEmotions
row/comment identifiers; the supplied restoration manifest records the source
and checksum. Model weights and inference executables are not distributed.
